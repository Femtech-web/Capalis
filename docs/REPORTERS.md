# For teammates who report bugs

You don't need to install anything or learn any commands.

1. **Post the bug in the agreed channel** (for example Discord **#bugs**), the way you'd tell a person. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.

   > The checkout button does nothing on Safari. Steps: add an item, open the cart, click Checkout. Expected: the payment page. Actual: nothing happens.

2. **You'll get a reply under your message within a few seconds:**

   > Thanks for the report. It is tracked as https://github.com/your-team/app/issues/12, and I will reply here when it is closed.

3. **When the team closes the issue, you'll get one more reply:**

   > Good news: https://github.com/your-team/app/issues/12 has been fixed and closed. Thanks for reporting it.

   If it was closed without a fix, the reply says so, along with the reason.

A few things to know:
- **Posting the same message again won't create a second issue.**
- **Only the people your team listed can trigger it.** Messages from anyone else are ignored and logged.
- **Your message text is copied into the issue as written.** Don't include passwords or tokens. Capalis also masks anything that looks like a credential before storing or forwarding it.
- **The ✅ under your message** is Hermes saying it received it. The replies are what confirm the work was done.
