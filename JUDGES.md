## Not claimed

- Slack incoming webhooks cannot read messages back, so Capalis reports them as unverifiable and never recommends them where a check is required.
- Only Capalis's reviewed adapters (GitHub Issues, Slack, HTTPS GET) can run in Recipes. Other Hermes tools are not executable until a contract test characterizes them.
- The event log is hash-chained to detect edits and deletions; it does not stop someone who can rewrite the whole database.
- Discord intake needs the channel listed in DISCORD_FREE_RESPONSE_CHANNELS so unmentioned messages reach Hermes.
- Reliability figures are run counts from your own runs, never percentages from scripted examples.
